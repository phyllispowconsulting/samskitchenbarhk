our story

Named after
Samuel

Located on Swatow Street in the heart of Wan Chai, Sam's Kitchen & Bar is a family-run neighbourhood restaurant inspired by our son, Samuel. Sam's reflects the values at the heart of everything we do: family, connection, hospitality, and creating a place where everyone feels welcome.

From breakfast through dinner, we serve a mix of Asian-inspired and Western favourites, including our signature bánh mì sandwiches, crafted using recipes and techniques generously shared by dear friends from Hong Kong's Vietnamese community.

Led by Chef Massimo and backed by years of hospitality experience, our kitchen focuses on honest, flavourful food made with care. At the bar, you'll find six beers on tap, a wide selection of bottled beers, and a small collection of well-crafted cocktails.
