About us

A family-run neighbourhood restaurant in the heart of Wan Chai.

Named after Samuel

Family, connection, hospitality

Located on Swatow Street in the heart of Wan Chai, Sam's Kitchen & Bar is a family-run neighbourhood restaurant inspired by our son, Samuel. Named after the person who means the most to us, Sam's reflects the values at the heart of everything we do: family, connection, hospitality, and creating a place where everyone feels welcome.

Our vision is simple: create a welcoming place where friends, families, neighbours, and visitors can come together over great food and good conversation.

from our kitchen

Led by
Chef Massimo

From breakfast through dinner, we serve a mix of Asian-inspired and Western favourites, including our signature bánh mì sandwiches, crafted using recipes and techniques generously shared by dear friends from Hong Kong's Vietnamese community.

Led by Chef Massimo and backed by years of hospitality experience, our kitchen focuses on honest, flavourful food made with care.
